Full-operator stable rank and squared-energy entropy rank after adding ALiBi-style distance penalties to fixed GPT-2 logits. All conditions use the same 32 frozen WikiText-103 documents, final layer 11, all 12 heads and nested native prefixes of 256, 512 and 1024 tokens. The slopes are m=0 (native), 0.01 and 0.1, applied uniformly across heads as controlled operator changes; GPT-2's native absolute-position representations remain fixed. Heads are averaged within each document, then documents are averaged; shaded bands are 95% percentile document-bootstrap intervals (2000 resamples, seed 20261108). Figure 1 reports head and document medians, so its baseline has a different aggregation. This figure concerns full stable rank and erank2 only; no cross-cut, value-gain or feedthrough measurement is included, and it is not a separately trained ALiBi checkpoint.
